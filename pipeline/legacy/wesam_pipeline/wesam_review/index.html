For threshold calibration, also mark at least three separate “Wesam validation” and three “Other-speaker validation” excerpts. Keep these out of enrollment. Scores are not probabilities; a low score does not confirm a guest’s identity.

The minimum controls which utterances get scored. Window length controls how much speech each embedding uses; hop controls how often it is computed. Scores below 1.5 seconds are marked as short estimates. Rerun identification after changing these settings.

Display rule: above the threshold = Wesam; below = other speaker; equal or no score = uncertain. This changes the labels on screen only; saved reports keep their original labels. Speech below the chosen minimum and detected overlap are not scored.
